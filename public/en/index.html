    Before you paste a schema
    
        
    What the tool will not do with your data.
    
        
    
          Can it change data in the database?
          
    No. Only one SELECT is allowed, with a forced limit and a filter on statements that modify data.
    
        
    
        
    
          Where do results go?
          
    To the browser. Results are not stored permanently.
    
        
    
        
    
          Do I need an account?
          
    Not for the demo or a one-off paste. An account helps when you want to keep the same schema. Upload a saved schema on Load schema.
    
        
    
        
    
          Where do I get a schema file?
          
    From a short query against MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Oracle, or SQLite. Ready-made examples are in the docs.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Schema export steps and the per-engine queries live outside the homepage, so the product comes first.
